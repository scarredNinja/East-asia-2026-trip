Trip Field Warnings & Calendar Traps

🇰🇷 Tue 20 Oct · Busan
Jagalchi Fish Market Closed

Regular 1st & 3rd Tuesday scheduled closure. Do Bupyeong Kkangtong Market or Seomyeon Gukbap instead on arrival evening.

🇯🇵 Wed 28 Oct · Kyoto
Kyoto Manga Museum Closed

Regular Wednesday weekly closure. Target Nijo Castle, Honno-ji, and Mibu-dera for your 28 Oct visit instead.

🇯🇵 Sat 31 Oct · Tokyo
Avoid Shibuya Halloween Night

Massive police roadblocks & alcohol bans. You are already booked in Kawasaki for Senbukai Karate (6:00–9:00 PM), avoiding this entirely.

🇯🇵 Mon 2 Nov · Tokyo
Monday Museum Closures

Japanese Sword Museum, Tokyo National Museum, and Shinjuku Gyoen all closed on Mondays. Channel into Kamakura or Nakano/Akiba.

🇯🇵 Tue 3 Nov · Tokyo
Culture Day (Bunka no Hi)

Public holiday. Meiji Jingu hosts traditional autumn martial arts demonstrations (kobudo and yabusame mounted archery).

🇯🇵 Wed 4 Nov · Departure
Kappabashi Knife Rule

If purchasing kitchen knives at Kappabashi near Asakusa, pack them inside checked bags before heading to Narita via Keisei Access Express!

🏨 Verified Base Accommodations & Doorstep Stations

🇰🇷 25–26 Oct Base Hongdae

3-28 Hongik-ro, Mapo-gu

Direct doorstep access to Hongik University Station Exit 9 (~300m walk).

Key Doorstep Routes:
  • To Incheon T2 (26 Oct): Direct AREX train from Hongik Univ. Stn straight to ICN Terminal 2 (58m, zero transfers). Board at 11:00 AM for KE0725.
  • To Euljiro / Hipjiro: Subway Line 2 direct to Euljiro 3-ga (14 mins, no transfer).
🇯🇵 26–30 Oct Base Naniwa-ku

1-2-10 Ebisunishi, Naniwa-ku

Positioned between Ebisucho Station Exit 5 (~150m) and Shin-Imamiya Station (~350m).

Key Doorstep Routes:
  • KIX Arrival (26 Oct): Nankai Airport Express direct to Shin-Imamiya (36 mins, 0 transfers). Walk 4m north.
  • South-Osaka Walk: Step right out into Shinsekai → Den Den Town (starts 300m away) → Namba → Dotonbori.
  • To Kyoto: Ebisucho Stn (Sakaisuji Line) → connect at Awaji to Hankyu Line → Kyoto-Kawaramachi (55 mins, ¥640).
  • To Tokyo Bullet Train: Dobutsuen-mae / Shin-Imamiya → Midosuji Line direct to Shin-Osaka (16 mins).
🎟️ SmartEX / Shinkansen Info
🇯🇵 30 Oct–4 Nov Base Asakusa

Asakusa Tobu Hotel (1-1-15 Hanakawado)

Directly opposite Asakusa Station (Tobu, Ginza Line, Toei Asakusa Line).

Key Doorstep Routes:
  • To Senbukai Karate (31 Oct): Toei Asakusa / Keikyu through-train toward Yokohama or Misakiguchi to Keikyu-Kawasaki (~36–45 min), then about 2 km / 27 min on foot or taxi to Senbukai Honbu Dojo, La Corte basement, 3-9-11 Nakajima, Kawasaki-ku. Avoid Haneda-bound trains.
  • To Hogyokukai Sword (1 Nov): 1–2 stops to Ryogoku / Kuramae area.
  • To Narita T1 (4 Nov): Keisei Access Express directly across the street from hotel to Narita T1 (1h 05m, 0 transfers).

🚆 Shinkansen & Transit Rules

🚨 Nozomi Oversized Luggage Rule:

For Fri 30 Oct Shin-Osaka → Tokyo bullet train, suitcases with total dimensions >160cm require a designated "Seat with Oversized Luggage Area" via SmartEX.

💳 IC Cards (ICOCA / Suica):

Usable interchangeably on subways and private trains in both Kansai and Tokyo. Tap directly for Ebisucho → Kyoto-Kawaramachi (¥640).

🇰🇷 South Korea Navigation:

Google Maps walking turn-by-turn is legally restricted in Korea. Use Naver Map or KakaoMap buttons provided for Busan and Seoul legs.
