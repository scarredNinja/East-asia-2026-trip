💡 Tactical Field Rules & Etiquette Notes

🔪 Kitchen Knife Packing Rule

Buying chef knives at Kappabashi Kitchen Street near Asakusa? Buy them on 4 Nov morning or keep them sealed and packed inside checked luggage. Never carry in hand luggage!

🎮 Subculture Timing

Osaka’s Den Den Town and Tokyo's Nakano Broadway / Akihabara shops typically open at 11:00 AM and shut by 8:00 PM. Dig for vinyl and figures before dinner.

🎧 Jazz Kissas & Listening Bars

Venues like Meikyoku Kissa Lion and Bar Martha maintain strict low-talking or whisper-only rules. Sit facing the speakers, order a pour-over or whiskey, and immerse without flash photography.

💳 Transit Cards & Navigation Apps Quick Guide Korea & Japan
🇰🇷 South Korea: Use T-Money or Cashbee card (buy/top-up at any convenience store like GS25 or CU). Note that Google Maps walking directions can be limited in Korea due to map data laws; use the integrated 🗺️ Map button to pin locations, or open Naver/Kakao Maps for complex pedestrian navigation.
🇯🇵 Japan: Tap your physical ICOCA / Suica / Pasmo or add an Apple Wallet digital Suica. Works on all subways, JR local trains, Hankyu lines to Kyoto, Keisei Access Express to Narita, and at conbini drink vending machines.
